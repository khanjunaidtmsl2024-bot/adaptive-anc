# SIH26052 — Detailed Master Study Guide

## How we will study every topic

Every major topic follows this sequence:

**1. Intuition**
What is physically happening?

**2. Terminology**
What does every variable/term mean?

**3. Mathematics**
Derive the important equations.

**4. Numerical examples**
Calculate things by hand.

**5. Visualization**
Waveforms/spectra/tables when useful.

**6. Coding**
Implement the concept ourselves.

**7. Real audio**
Run it on actual signals.

**8. Hardware connection**
Where does it exist in our prototype?

**9. Failure modes**
What can go wrong?

**10. Engineering decision**
What would *you* choose and why?

**11. Experiment**
Measure it.

**12. Mastery checkpoint**
You explain/derive/debug it without depending on AI.

That is the standard.

Learn:

$$
y[n]=x[n]*h[n]
$$

$$
y[n]=\sum_k x[k]h[n-k]
$$

## 5.4 Why convolution matters

Because a huge amount of your project can eventually be understood as:

$$
\boxed{\text{signal} * \text{system response}}
$$

That includes acoustic paths.

Your ANC secondary path is fundamentally related to this concept.

# Where we are now

You are currently at:

$$
\boxed{\text{PHASE 4 — SYSTEMS}}
$$

and specifically:

$$
\boxed{\text{LINEARITY}}
$$

We've established that:

$$
y[n]=4x[n]-2x[n-1]
$$

is linear despite having memory.

**Our next lesson should therefore be a deeper treatment of linearity**, including the zero-input test, superposition test, several tricky examples, and—most importantly—**how linearity differs from time invariance**.

Then we'll move to **time invariance**.

We are **not skipping ahead to AI**. That would put you back into the exact dependency problem that is frustrating you now.
